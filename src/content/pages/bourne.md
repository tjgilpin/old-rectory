## Cosy & Private

Enjoy a cosy night in the private lounge with an equipped kitchen and dining space. Decorated throughout with wallpaper by Deborah Bowness each room is unique, creating a home away from home. This apartment space can sleep up to four guests and has views of both the garden & the churchyard.

#### This room features:

* A double bed with feather bedding & high quality cotton sheets facing the garden
* A double bed with feather bedding & high quality cotton sheets facing the church-yard 
* A sizeable shower room  
* Tea & Coffee from Ringtons & homemade biscuits 
* An equipped kitchen with electric hob, oven & toaster
* Clock radio, smart Tv, unlimited Wi-Fi
* Portable fan and heaters 
* Hypoallergenic bedding available on request
* From £180 including breakfast

#### Family Friendly Options:

Enjoy all of these features with your family. Bourne is now open to families with young ones, aged three and above, and also to those travelling with their dogs. There are just a few extra Terms and Conditions associated with these types of bookings to help keep everyone safe and to ensure that all guests are still treated to the classic Rectory experience.

These bookings must adhere to the following policies:

● Young children and pets are not permitted to use the communal guest lounges.

● Young children are not permitted to use the garden unless accompanied by an adult.

● Dogs must be kept on a lead when in the garden.

● Young children and dogs are not to be left in the flat unattended for any period of time.

● Breakfast will be brought up to your kitchen for you to enjoy. We will ask you to pre-order

with us the day before.

● Any bookings with pets are subject to a £35 cleaning fee per stay.
